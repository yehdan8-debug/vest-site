The idea

You track your money. Why not your life?

Vest rolls your daily actions across five life domains into one Value Score. Do the things that matter and it climbs; let them slide and it eases back. A calm, honest signal of how you're really doing, updated like a market you're invested in.

2

Create habits that fit your life. Every one you complete nudges its domain, and your Value Score, upward.
