Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data. You can also request deletion by emailing us.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through Apple. Manage or cancel anytime from your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Vest.

How do I report or block another user?

Open their profile from People or a friend's page and tap the "⋯" menu for Report or Block. We review every report and commit to taking appropriate action within 24 hours. You can also report by email, see below.

How do I report content or a user without the app?

Email support@getvest.app with as much detail as you can: what or who you're reporting, and when you encountered it. We honour the same 24-hour response commitment for reports made this way.
